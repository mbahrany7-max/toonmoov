Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

ToonMoov makes short animated films and publishes them on its own YouTube channel and Instagram account. This policy covers the ToonMoov website and the small ToonMoov tools that manage our own channel through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

What the ToonMoov tools access

Our tools sign in only to the ToonMoov channel, with the channel owner's permission given through Google sign-in. They use the YouTube API Services to upload and schedule our own videos, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read our own channel's statistics. They are not offered to other people and do not access anyone else's account.

What we store

The sign-in token and the channel numbers we read are kept on the channel owner's own computers. We do not sell, rent or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising. The data is used only to run and improve the ToonMoov channel.

Visitors to this website

This website does not use cookies, does not ask for any personal information and runs no analytics or advertising of its own. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

YouTube

Our tools use YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access given to the ToonMoov tools can be removed at any time on the Google account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is removed, the stored token stops working and we delete it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @toonmoov on Instagram.
